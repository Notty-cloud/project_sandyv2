# Project Sandy — Storyboard & Production Package
**"FR Attendance"** · Total Runtime: 5:00 · 8 Scenes

---

## Full Narration Script

**Scene 1 — Meet Sandy (0:00 – 0:35)**
Hi, I'm Ms. Clarke — and I want to show you something that has completely changed the way our school takes attendance.
Every day counts. Every student matters. But for too long, schools have been tracking attendance the hard way — paper registers, roll calls, names missed, time wasted.
That ends today.
This is Sandy — FR Attendance. Fast. Accurate. Automatic. From the very first day.
No more paper. No more errors. No more missing students.
Let me show you exactly how it works.

**Scene 2 — The Login Page (0:35 – 1:15)**
Welcome to FR Attendance — the School Attendance Facial Recognition System.
Getting in is simple. Enter your username, enter your password, and hit Sign In.
Now — not everyone has the same access. We have three roles in this system.
First — the Admin, also known as the Head Teacher. Full control of everything.
Then we have the Coordinator. That's me. I manage the day-to-day — attendance, enrollment, and overrides.
And finally — the Teacher. They view attendance for their own classes. Clean and simple.
Three roles. One system. Let's go inside.

**Scene 3 — Student Management (1:15 – 1:55)**
Before Sandy can recognize anyone — they need to be in the system.
And that starts right here. Student Management.
Adding a student is simple. Click Add Student — a clean window opens up.
First — the student's full name. Let's say Druell Alstrom.
Next — a Student ID. We assign STU-003. Every student gets their own unique ID.
Then we select the Grade — Sandy supports Grade 7 through Grade 12.
And finally the Section — A, B, C, D, or E.
Hit Add Student — and just like that, Druell Alstrom is in the system. Ready to be enrolled. Ready to be recognized.
No paperwork. No filing. One form. Done.

**Scene 4 — Enrollment (1:55 – 2:40)**
Now that Druell is in the system — it's time to enroll them.
We head over to the Enrollment Hub. Right next to the student's name, you'll see a small camera icon. That's your enrollment button. Click it.
A window opens up instantly. Two options at the top — Live Camera or Existing Image. We're using Live Camera.
Now here's the important part. You'll see a circle in the middle of the screen. The student needs to look directly at the camera — face forward, clean background, no blur, no shadows.
Centre the face inside that circle. When it looks good — click Take Photo.
Sandy captures the image and processes it immediately. Quality — 100%. Embedding — Generated. That means Sandy has learned this face.
Then hit Enrol Student — and it's done.
One photo. Enrolled forever.

**Scene 5 — Students Enrolled (2:40 – 3:15)**
And just like that — look at this!
Five students. All added. All active. All ready to go.
At the top, our numbers at a glance. Total Students — 5. Active — 5. Inactive — zero. Nobody sitting on the bench today!
We've got Druell Alstrom, Grade 10, Section A. Korin Brewster, Grade 10, Section A. June Smith, Grade 9, Section B. And Nathan Grimes, Grade 9, Section B. All active. All ready.
Every student has a photo count — 1 out of 5 so far — which means Sandy is already learning their faces.
Need to find someone? Search by name. Filter by grade. Filter by section. Any student — found in seconds.
Your entire student database — organised, live, and always up to date.

**Scene 6 — Manual Override (3:15 – 3:55)**
Now — Sandy is smart. But Sandy isn't perfect. Sometimes facial recognition fails. Maybe the lighting was off. Maybe the camera didn't pick up the face. It happens.
That's where Manual Override comes in.
Right here on this page, we can see exactly what went wrong. Total Failures — 2. Low Confidence — 1. No Face Detected — 1. Already Overridden — 0.
Sandy tells us why each recognition failed — so we know exactly how to fix it.
We can filter by class. Filter by date. Pull up any record in seconds.
See here — Korin Brewster, Grade 10A, low confidence at 62%. June Smith, no face detected. Nathan Grimes, low confidence at 58%.
One click on Override — and a coordinator steps in to correct it manually.
And every single change is logged. Tracked. Documented. Because schools need a full audit trail — and Sandy makes sure you have one.
Manual control when the AI needs help. Accurate. Traceable. Ready for audit.

**Scene 7 — The Dashboard (3:55 – 4:30)**
And it all comes together — right here. The Dashboard.
This is Sandy's command center. At a glance — it tells you everything that matters.
Total Classes. Active Classes. Pending Enrollments. Failed Registrations. All tracked in real time.
From this one screen, you have three powerful quick-access buttons.
View Class Attendance — see real-time records and mark students present.
Register New Students — go straight to enrollment.
And Handle Failed Registrations — jump into Manual Override and fix anything that needs your attention.
Everything important. In one place. No digging. No confusion. No wasted time.
One dashboard. Fast decisions. Smooth school operations.

**Scene 8 — The Future (4:30 – 5:00)**
Sandy was built from scratch — by six students, one team, one shared goal.
React. Django. DeepFace AI. Months of code, real testing, and genuine collaboration — for classrooms that still run on paper.
Our mission is to bring Sandy to every school in Barbados. Alma Parris. Ellerslie Secondary. Every student. Every morning.
Because no student should ever be missed.
Every face matters.
Every day counts.
This is Project Sandy.

## How to Produce the Video

### Tools
| Tool | Purpose |
|---|---|
| ElevenLabs | Generate voice-over narration per scene |
| OBS Studio / Windows Game Bar (Win+G) | Screen-record the Sandy app for scenes 2–7 |
| Runway ML / Pika Labs / Kling AI | Generate cinematic footage for scenes 1, 8, 9 |
| CapCut (desktop) | Edit, trim, add captions, sync audio |
| YouTube Audio Library | Background music — copyright-safe and free |

### Workflow
1. Record narration per scene in ElevenLabs using settings above
2. Screen-record Sandy app walkthroughs for scenes 2–7
3. Generate AI video clips for scenes 1, 8, and 9 using the visual descriptions above
4. Import everything into CapCut — arrange per timecodes
5. Add captions as text overlays using the caption sequences
6. Add soft background music under all scenes
7. Export at 1080p
